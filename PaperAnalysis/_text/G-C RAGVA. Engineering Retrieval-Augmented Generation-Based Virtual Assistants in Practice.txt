Keywords:                       Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)-based applications are gaining prominence due to their ability to
Retrieval augmented generation  leverage large language models (LLMs). These systems excel at combining retrieval mechanisms with gen-
Virtual assistants              erative capabilities, resulting in contextually relevant responses that enhance user experience. In particular,
Software engineering            Transurban, a road operation company, replaced its rule-based virtual assistant (VA) with a RAG-based VA
AI engineering                  (RAGVA) to offer flexible customer interactions and support a wider range of scenarios. This paper presents
SE4AI                           an experience report from Transurban's engineering team on building and deploying a RAGVA, offering a
LLMOps                          step-by-step guide for creating a conversational application and engineering a RAGVA. The report serves as
Responsible AI                  a reference for future researchers and practitioners. While the engineering processes for traditional software
                                applications are well-established, the development and evaluation of RAG-based applications are still in their
                                early stages, with numerous emerging challenges remaining uncharted. To address this gap, we conduct a focus
                                group study with Transurban practitioners regarding developing and evaluating their RAGVA. We identified
                                eight challenges encountered by the engineering team and proposed eight future directions that should be
                                explored to advance the development of RAG-based applications. This study contributes to the foundational
                                understanding of a RAG-based conversational application and the emerging AI software engineering challenges
                                it presents.
